Chapter III

The Curriculum

  1. Diagnose

    Run needs analysis and skill gap assessments with the people doing the work and the leaders measuring it. Build learner personas and map their journey, so the training fits who they are and where they get stuck.

  2. Design

    Choose the mix of instructor-led, virtual and self-paced learning, grounded in adult learning and universal design for learning.

  3. Build and Review

    Build with subject matter experts in Articulate 360 and Brightspace, and audit every piece against AODA accessibility standards.

  4. Measure

    Track participation, completion and comprehension on a dashboard, then revise what the data shows is not working.
