Headings and Structure

Clear structure lets every learner find their place, whether they scan with their eyes or with a screen reader.

Why Structure Matters

People who use screen readers often jump from heading to heading to find what they need. Sighted learners scan headings the same way. Real headings help both.

Four Habits

  • Use one main heading per page, then go down one level at a time. Do not skip from level 1 to level 3.
  • Use the heading styles in your authoring tool. Large bold text looks like a heading but is not announced as one.
  • Use real bulleted and numbered lists, so the number of items is announced.
  • Write link text that makes sense on its own.
